On'yomi Examples ⓘ On'yomi (音読み) — "Sound reading"

The Chinese-derived pronunciation, brought to Japan with kanji characters. Written in katakana in dictionaries.

When used: Mostly in compound words (熟語) made of multiple kanji, like 食事 (しょくじ, meal).

Tip: Some words mix readings! 時計 (とけい, clock) uses kun + on — called jūbako-yomi (重箱読み). (ショク)
食事 (しょくじ) meal
食事の時間です。
Shokuji no jikan desu.
It's meal time.
Kun'yomi Examples ⓘ Kun'yomi (訓読み) — "Meaning reading"

The native Japanese pronunciation that existed before kanji arrived. Written in hiragana in dictionaries.

When used: Often when kanji stands alone or with hiragana (okurigana), like 食べる (たべる, to eat).

Tip: Verbs and adjectives usually use kun'yomi with hiragana endings that show conjugation. (た・べる)
食べる (たべる) to eat
朝ごはんを食べます。
Asa-gohan wo tabemasu.
I eat breakfast.
