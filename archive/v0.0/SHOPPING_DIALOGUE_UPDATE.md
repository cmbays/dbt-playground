## 🎯 Dialogue Content Overview

### 1. Present Tense - Supermarket Shopping

**Scenario:** Shopping for vegetables at a supermarket

**Conversation Flow:**
1. Customer asks where vegetable section is
2. Staff directs to location (back of first floor, next to fruit)
3. Customer asks if tomatoes are fresh
4. Staff confirms they just arrived this morning
5. Customer asks price
6. Staff mentions sale: 300 yen/pack, or 2 for 500 yen
7. Customer buys two packs

**Grammar Tip:**
- い-adjectives: 安**い** (cheap), 高**い** (expensive)
- な-adjectives: 便利**な** (convenient), 不便**な** (inconvenient)

**Key Vocabulary:**
- 野菜売り場 (yasai uriba) - vegetable section
- 新鮮 (shinsen) - fresh
- 特売日 (tokubai bi) - sale day

**Kanji:** 15 characters including 野菜売場階果物鮮朝入荷特日買安

---

### 2. Past Tense - 100 Yen Shop

**Scenario:** Discussing a past shopping trip to a 100 yen shop

**Conversation Flow:**
1. Customer mentions going to 100 yen shop yesterday
2. Friend asks what they bought
3. Customer lists: notebook, pens, small towel (500 yen total)
4. Friend asks about quality
5. Customer says it was better than expected, pens were light and easy to write with

**Grammar Tip:**
- Past tense adjectives: い → かった (高い → 高**かった**)
- な-adjectives use でした (便利 → 便利**でした**)

**Key Vocabulary:**
- 百円ショップ (hyaku en shoppu) - 100 yen shop
- 品質 (hinshitsu) - quality
- 全部で (zenbu de) - in total

**Cultural Note:** 100 yen shops (like Daiso, Seria) are incredibly popular in Japan for daily necessities

**Kanji:** 11 characters including 百円何全部品質思軽書

---

### 3. Future Tense - Electronics Store

**Scenario:** Planning to buy a laptop at an electronics store in Akihabara

**Conversation Flow:**
1. Customer plans to go to Akihabara next week for laptop
2. Friend asks if buying online or in-store
3. Customer wants to see and try in person first
4. Friend says Akihabara has many shops, recommends comparing
5. Customer will look at several stores and check specs
6. Friend mentions online is cheaper
7. Customer agrees but wants to try keyboard and screen first

**Grammar Tip:**
- Future intentions: ～つもりです (intend to), ～予定です (plan to)
- Comparisons: ～より (than), もっと (more)

**Key Vocabulary:**
- 来週 (raishuu) - next week
- 電気屋 (denkiya) - electronics store
- 秋葉原 (Akihabara) - Tokyo's electronics district
- 実際に (jissai ni) - actually, in person

**Cultural Note:** Akihabara is Tokyo's famous "Electric Town" with countless electronics stores

**Kanji:** 14 characters including 来週電気屋種詳確実決重感認賢

---

### 4. Advanced - Clothing Store with Keigo

**Scenario:** Shopping for a coat at a department store using polite language

**Conversation Flow:**
1. Staff offers help with polite keigo
2. Customer asks about coat in window
3. Staff asks which one (right side blue one)
4. Customer wants to see and try it on
5. Staff confirms size (M) and leads to fitting room
6. Customer tries it on, fits well
7. Asks about other colors
8. Staff shows black option
9. Customer decides on blue, asks to purchase
10. Staff thanks and processes purchase

**Grammar Tip:**
- Conditional forms: ～たら (if/when), ～なら (if)
- Polite requests: ～ていただけますか (could you please...?)
- Keigo: いらっしゃいませ (welcome), かしこまりました (certainly)

**Key Vocabulary:**
- いらっしゃいませ (irasshaimase) - welcome (keigo)
- 試着室 (shichakushitsu) - fitting room
- かしこまりました (kashikomarimashita) - certainly (keigo)
- 少々お待ちください (shoushou omachi kudasai) - please wait a moment

**Cultural Note:** Japanese retail service uses very polite keigo language

**Kanji:** 13 characters including 試着室合少色黒見申訳切取置
