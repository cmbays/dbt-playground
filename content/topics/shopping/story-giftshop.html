🎁 京都でお土産を買う (Buying Omiyage Souvenirs in Kyoto)

中村さんは京都を旅行していました。明日東京に帰るので、家族や同僚のためにお土産を買う必要がありました。清水寺の近くにある有名なお土産屋さんに入りました。店内にはたくさんの伝統的な商品が並んでいました。

Nakamura-san wa Kyouto wo ryokou shite imashita. Ashita Toukyou ni kaeru node, kazoku ya douryou no tame ni omiyage wo kau hitsuyou ga arimashita. Kiyomizu-dera no chikaku ni aru yuumei na omiyage-ya-san ni hairimashita. Tennai ni wa takusan no dentou-teki na shouhin ga narande imashita. • Ms. Nakamura was traveling in Kyoto. Since she was returning to Tokyo tomorrow, she needed to buy souvenirs for her family and colleagues. She entered a famous souvenir shop near Kiyomizu Temple. Many traditional products were lined up inside the store.
🎌 Cultural Note: お土産 (omiyage) is an important part of Japanese culture. When traveling, it's customary to buy omiyage for family, friends, and coworkers. These are typically regional specialties or local sweets, nicely packaged. Giving omiyage shows thoughtfulness and maintains social harmony.

まず、お菓子のコーナーを見ました。八つ橋や抹茶のお菓子が人気でした。店員さんが試食をすすめてくれました。「こちらの抹茶チョコレートはいかがですか。とても人気がありますよ」と説明してくれました。味見をすると、とても美味しかったです。

Mazu, okashi no koonaa wo mimashita. Yatsuhashi ya matcha no okashi ga ninki deshita. Ten'in-san ga shishoku wo susumete kuremashita. "Kochira no matcha chokoreeto wa ikaga desu ka. Totemo ninki ga arimasu yo" to setsumei shite kuremashita. Ajimi wo suru to, totemo oishikatta desu. • First, she looked at the sweets corner. Yatsuhashi and matcha sweets were popular. The staff recommended a sample. She explained, "How about this matcha chocolate? It's very popular." When she tasted it, it was very delicious.
📝 Grammar Tip: ～てくれる (te kureru) shows someone did something for your benefit: すすめてくれました (susumete kuremashita - "recommended for me").

同僚のために、個包装のお菓子を二箱買いました。一箱に二十個入っているので、みんなで分けられます。次に、家族のために別の物を探しました。母には扇子、父には湯呑み、妹には可愛い和柄の小物を選びました。

Douryou no tame ni, kobousou no okashi wo ni-hako kaimashita. Ichi-hako ni nijuu-ko haitte iru node, minna de wakeraremasu. Tsugi ni, kazoku no tame ni betsu no mono wo sagashimashita. Haha ni wa sensu, chichi ni wa yunomi, imouto ni wa kawaii wagara no komono wo erabimashita. • For her colleagues, she bought two boxes of individually wrapped sweets. Since there are 20 pieces in one box, everyone can share them. Next, she looked for different things for her family. She chose a folding fan for her mother, a teacup for her father, and cute Japanese-patterned accessories for her younger sister.
📝 Grammar Tip: ～ために (tame ni) means "for the sake of" or "for": 同僚のために (douryou no tame ni - "for colleagues").

お土産を全部選び終わると、店員が「こちらのお土産セットもおすすめです」と教えてくれました。京都の名産品が入ったセットで、お得でした。一つ追加で買いました。全部で一万二千円になりました。

Omiyage wo zenbu erabi owaru to, ten'in ga "kochira no omiyage setto mo osusume desu" to oshiete kuremashita. Kyouto no meisan-hin ga haitta setto de, otoku deshita. Hitotsu tsuika de kaimashita. Zenbu de ichi-man ni-sen-en ni narimashita. • When she finished selecting all the souvenirs, the staff told her "This souvenir set is also recommended." It was a set containing Kyoto specialties and was a good deal. She bought one more. The total came to 12,000 yen.
🎌 Cultural Note: 名産品 (meisan-hin - local specialties) are highly valued as omiyage. Each region of Japan has unique products: Kyoto is famous for 八つ橋 (yatsuhashi rice crackers), 抹茶 (matcha) items, and traditional crafts. Individually wrapped items (個包装 kobousou) are preferred for office gifts.

レジで支払いをする時、店員が「ラッピングは無料でいたします。それぞれ別々に包装しましょうか」と聞きました。中村さんは「家族用とオフィス用を分けてください」とお願いしました。店員は丁寧に包装してくれました。

Reji de shiharai wo suru toki, ten'in ga "rappingu wa muryou de itashimasu. Sorezore betsubetsu ni housou shimashou ka" to kikimashita. Nakamura-san wa "kazoku-you to ofisu-you wo wakete kudasai" to onegai shimashita. Ten'in wa teinei ni housou shite kuremashita. • When paying at the register, the staff asked "We provide wrapping for free. Shall we wrap each separately?" Ms. Nakamura requested, "Please separate the ones for family and for the office." The staff carefully wrapped them.
📝 Grammar Tip: ～てください (te kudasai) is a polite request: 分けてください (wakete kudasai - "please separate").

買い物が終わって、重い袋を受け取りました。店員が「お気をつけてお帰りください。京都のお土産を喜んでいただけると嬉しいです」と笑顔で見送ってくれました。中村さんは、素敵なお土産を買えて満足しました。みんなに喜んでもらえるだろうと思いました。

Kaimono ga owatte, omoi fukuro wo uketorimashita. Ten'in ga "oki wo tsukete okaeri kudasai. Kyouto no omiyage wo yorokonde itadakeru to ureshii desu" to egao de miokutte kuremashita. Nakamura-san wa, suteki na omiyage wo kaete manzoku shimashita. Minna ni yorokonde moraeru darou to omoimashita. • After finishing shopping, she received the heavy bags. The staff saw her off with a smile saying "Please take care on your way back. We'd be happy if you enjoy the Kyoto souvenirs." Ms. Nakamura was satisfied having bought wonderful souvenirs. She thought everyone would be pleased.
