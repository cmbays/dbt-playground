1. PRESENT TENSE - SUPERMARKET SHOPPING
   ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Scenario:  Shopping for fresh vegetables
   Lines:     7 exchanges

   Content:
   • Customer asks where vegetable section is
   • Staff provides directions
   • Discussion about freshness and pricing
   • Sale offer: 2 packs for 500 yen

   Grammar Tip:
   💡 い-adjectives vs な-adjectives
      安い (cheap), 高い (expensive)
      便利な (convenient), 不便な (inconvenient)

   Key Phrases:
   • すみません、野菜売り場はどこですか
     (Excuse me, where is the vegetable section?)
   • このトマトは新鮮ですか
     (Are these tomatoes fresh?)
   • いくらですか
     (How much is it?)

   Kanji: 野菜売場階果物鮮朝入荷特日買安 (15 kanji)


2. PAST TENSE - 100 YEN SHOP
   ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Scenario:  Discussing yesterday's shopping trip
   Lines:     5 exchanges

   Content:
   • Telling friend about 100 yen shop visit
   • Listing items purchased (notebook, pens, towel)
   • Discussing quality and value
   • Total cost: 500 yen

   Grammar Tip:
   💡 Past tense adjectives
      い → かった: 高い → 高かった
      な + でした: 便利 → 便利でした

   Key Phrases:
   • 昨日、百円ショップに行きました
     (Yesterday, I went to a 100 yen shop)
   • 何を買いましたか
     (What did you buy?)
   • 思ったより良かったです
     (It was better than I thought)

   Cultural Note: 100 yen shops (Daiso, Seria) are extremely
                  popular for daily necessities in Japan

   Kanji: 百円何全部品質思軽書 (11 kanji)


3. FUTURE TENSE - ELECTRONICS STORE
   ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Scenario:  Planning laptop purchase in Akihabara
   Lines:     7 exchanges

   Content:
   • Plans to visit Akihabara next week
   • Comparing online vs in-store shopping
   • Importance of trying before buying
   • Checking specs and prices

   Grammar Tip:
   💡 Future intentions and comparisons
      ～つもりです (intend to)
      ～予定です (plan to)
      ～より (than), もっと (more)

   Key Phrases:
   • 来週秋葉原の電気屋に行くつもりです
     (I plan to go to electronics store in Akihabara next week)
   • 実際に見て試したいです
     (I want to see and try it in person)
   • 色々な店を見て、比べます
     (I'll look at various stores and compare)

   Cultural Note: Akihabara is Tokyo's famous "Electric Town"
                  with countless electronics stores

   Kanji: 来週電気屋種詳確実決重感認賢 (14 kanji)


4. ADVANCED - CLOTHING STORE (KEIGO)
   ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Scenario:  Buying a coat with polite business language
   Lines:     10 exchanges

   Content:
   • Staff greets with keigo (いらっしゃいませ)
   • Customer asks about window display coat
   • Trying on in fitting room
   • Discussing size and color options
   • Polite purchase completion

   Grammar Tip:
   💡 Conditional forms and polite requests
      ～たら (if/when), ～なら (if)
      ～ていただけますか (could you please...?)
      Keigo: いらっしゃいませ, かしこまりました

   Key Phrases:
   • いらっしゃいませ。何かお探しでしょうか
     (Welcome. Are you looking for something?)
   • 試着していただけますか
     (Could I try it on?)
   • かしこまりました。少々お待ちください
     (Certainly. Please wait a moment)
   • 他の色もございますか
     (Do you have other colors?)

   Cultural Note: Japanese retail uses very polite keigo
                  Standard phrases are expected

   Kanji: 試着室合少色黒見申訳切取置 (13 kanji)
