🛒 スーパーマーケットでの買い物 (Grocery Shopping at a Supermarket)

週末、山田さんは近所のスーパーマーケットに行きました。午前中に買い物をするのが好きで、この時間帯は野菜が新鮮です。入口でショッピングカートを取り、店内に入りました。

Shuumatsu, Yamada-san wa kinjo no suupaamaaketto ni ikimashita. Gozen-chuu ni kaimono wo suru no ga suki de, kono jikantai wa yasai ga shinsen desu. Iriguchi de shoppingu kaato wo tori, tennai ni hairimashita. • On the weekend, Ms. Yamada went to the nearby supermarket. She likes shopping in the morning, and vegetables are fresh during this time. She took a shopping cart at the entrance and entered the store.
🎌 Cultural Note: Japanese supermarkets (スーパー suupaa) pride themselves on freshness. Many shoppers go early in the morning when produce is freshest, and stores often discount items approaching their sell-by date in the evening with special 割引 (waribiki - discount) stickers.

最初に青果売り場で野菜と果物を選びました。トマト、きゅうり、人参を買いました。リンゴも安かったので、五つ買いました。店員さんが「このリンゴは甘くて美味しいですよ」と教えてくれました。

Saisho ni seika uriba de yasai to kudamono wo erabimashita. Tomato, kyuuri, ninjin wo kaimashita. Ringo mo yasukatta node, itsutsu kaimashita. Ten'in-san ga "kono ringo wa amakute oishii desu yo" to oshiete kuremashita. • First, she selected vegetables and fruits in the produce section. She bought tomatoes, cucumbers, and carrots. Apples were also cheap, so she bought five. The staff member told her, "These apples are sweet and delicious."
📝 Grammar Tip: ～てくれる (te kureru) shows that someone does something for you as a favor: 教えてくれました (oshiete kuremashita - "told me/taught me [for my benefit]").

次に精肉コーナーに行きました。今晩の夕食用に鶏肉を買いました。魚売り場では、新鮮なサーモンと鯖を見つけました。「今日の魚はどれが新鮮ですか」と店員に聞いてみました。

Tsugi ni seiniku koonaa ni ikimashita. Konban no yuushoku-you ni toriniku wo kaimashita. Sakana uriba de wa, shinsen na saamon to saba wo mitsukemashita. "Kyou no sakana wa dore ga shinsen desu ka" to ten'in ni kiite mimashita. • Next, she went to the meat corner. She bought chicken for tonight's dinner. At the fish section, she found fresh salmon and mackerel. She tried asking the staff, "Which fish is fresh today?"

乳製品コーナーで牛乳、チーズ、ヨーグルトを取りました。パンや卵も必要だったので、それぞれの売り場を回りました。調味料売り場では、醤油と味噌を補充しました。

Nyuuseihin koonaa de gyuunyuu, chiizu, yooguruto wo torimashita. Pan ya tamago mo hitsuyou datta node, sorezore no uriba wo mawarimashita. Choumiryou uriba de wa, shouyu to miso wo hojuu shimashita. • At the dairy section, she took milk, cheese, and yogurt. Since bread and eggs were also needed, she went around to each section. At the condiments section, she restocked soy sauce and miso.
📝 Grammar Tip: ～ので (node) means "because" and connects cause and effect naturally: 必要だったので (hitsuyou datta node - "because it was necessary").

買い物が終わって、レジに並びました。前に三人のお客さんがいました。自分の番が来ると、店員さんが商品をスキャンしてくれました。「ポイントカードはお持ちですか」と聞かれたので、カードを見せました。

Kaimono ga owatte, reji ni narabimashita. Mae ni san-nin no okyaku-san ga imashita. Jibun no ban ga kuru to, ten'in-san ga shouhin wo sukyan shite kuremashita. "Pointo kaado wa omochi desu ka" to kikareta node, kaado wo misemashita. • After finishing shopping, she lined up at the register. There were three customers ahead of her. When her turn came, the staff member scanned her items. When asked "Do you have a point card?", she showed her card.
🎌 Cultural Note: Point cards (ポイントカード pointo kaado) are extremely popular in Japan. Almost every store has a loyalty program where customers earn points with purchases. Staff always ask "ポイントカードはお持ちですか" (Do you have a point card?) at checkout.

合計金額は四千五百円でした。現金で支払いをして、レシートと釣り銭を受け取りました。自分でエコバッグに商品を詰めて、店を出ました。新鮮な食材を買えて満足でした。

Goukei kingaku wa yon-sen go-hyaku-en deshita. Genkin de shiharai wo shite, reshiito to tsurisen wo uketorimashita. Jibun de eko baggu ni shouhin wo tsumete, mise wo demashita. Shinsen na shokuzai wo kaete manzoku deshita. • The total amount was 4,500 yen. She paid with cash and received the receipt and change. She packed the items into her eco-bag herself and left the store. She was satisfied with buying fresh ingredients.
📝 Grammar Tip: ～て (te form) can show satisfaction about a completed action: 買えて満足でした (kaete manzoku deshita - "was satisfied having bought").
